# Lead folder, arc 2026-11-13

This folder is the lead's working record for the 2026-11-13 arc: the plan, the index of routes examined, the running log, the briefs given to the sessions, and the desk memos and surveys those sessions produced. Nothing here is pushed. Experiments themselves live on their own branches under `research/experiments/<name>/`.

## Contents

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| THESIS_PLAN.md | Thesis plan, draft 2: the question, where the project stands in numbers, the obstruction, research questions, papers, next steps. Section 9 holds what the day added and supersedes sections 4, 7 and 8 where they differ. |
| ROUTES.md | Index of every way of attacking Eternity II already examined in the repo, with verdicts copied from the judges. A brief for a new track must cite its row here. |
| STATUS.md | Dated log of the arc (owner instructions, track briefings, outcomes, judge results). |
| DRAFTS.md | Drafts of ledger entries and similar text, not applied to the repo. |
| DOCS_BRIEF.md | Common brief for the documentation sessions (shared worktree, file ownership, commit rules). |
| GAPS_BRIEF.md | Common brief for the seven desk sessions, one per gap in the working explanation of the 2^134.4 node cost. |
| SURVEY_BRIEF.md | Common brief for the field surveys: map what other fields know about problems of this structure. |
| memos/ | Output of the gap sessions and related desk work: one memo per gap (gap-1 to gap-6b), how-the-puzzle-was-made, nogood-frame-search-price, the d3 and d4 design reviews, a `.REVIEW.md` for most, and the scripts and outputs behind each. |
| surveys/ | One survey per field (exact algorithms, learning-guided search, Monte Carlo rare events, statistical physics, tilings, SAT and proof complexity, cryptanalysis and coding, quantum, the Seifer 2026 reading), plus citation and derivation reviews. |

## Reading order

1. THESIS_PLAN.md, section 9 first (the current position), then the earlier sections for context.
2. ROUTES.md (what has already been tried, and what looks untried).
3. STATUS.md as the log, to trace how each result was reached.
4. The briefs, memos and surveys as needed.
